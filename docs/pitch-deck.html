Problem

Caribbean SMEs run on chaos stacks

Today’s reality

  • WhatsApp for orders & customer chat
  • Spreadsheets for stock & jobs
  • Notebooks for expenses
  • Separate tools for invoicing

What breaks

  • Lost information between chats & books
  • Late invoices and weak collections
  • No clear cash-flow visibility
  • Hard to know if a job actually made money

Enterprise ERPs are too expensive and too complex. Generic global tools ignore TTD pricing, local VAT, and how Caribbean service + retail shops actually work.
